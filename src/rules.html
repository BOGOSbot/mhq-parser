Army-list parser Wahapedia

The question is searched against an indexed copy of the current-edition rules, not answered from memory. Both halves are searched — core rules, and faction datasheets, stratagems, detachments and enhancements:
  1. translate — a French question becomes English search terms first, because the corpus is English;
  2. retrieve — each half is searched separately, then jev free reranks the union. Jev decides; it does not write;
  3. judge + answer — the findings below are the product; a small model writes alongside them and judges whether they answer the question, and the two do not have to be the same model.
  4. voice is optional: the Voice button transcribes in your browser, French or English, and needs https or localhost. The text is left in the box to correct — speech recognition mangles unit names, and the wrong unit retrieves the wrong rule.
Every citation below links to the section it came from — …/core-rules/#1.-Hit-Roll or a datasheet page. The anchor is derived by the server from the heading, never written by the model, so a citation cannot point at a source that was not indexed.
