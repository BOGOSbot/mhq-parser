# Points verification — BOGOS Team : 6 dés sous terre (2026-10-10)

Reference: https://mfm.warhammer-community.com/en — Munitorum Field Manual v1.5, scraped per faction (20 pages: every datasheet in the lists plus the ally pages actually used).

## Method

484 unit rows across 35 lists. Each row: (1) datasheet matched to the English MFM page — French roster names translated; (2) printed figure checked for membership in the MFM tier/bracket tables (first-2nd vs 3rd+ copy pricing, recent ▲/▼ updates included); (3) on a miss, checked whether the delta equals the unit's own enhancement (app/newrecruit both fold it into the figure) or a multiple of the row's MFM wargear-option prices.

## Systemic finding

The recurring small deltas across the event stem from three legitimate sources — app folded-in wargear options, folded-in enhancement costs, and the recent MFM update wave (Lokhust Destroyers +15, Nekrosor +10, Logan +50, Doomsday Ark −10...). One thing this scan confirms: **thepressor parsed totals match the MFM once enhancement/wargear pricing is accounted for**, so a `points mismatch` warning from this parser is either a genuine roster error or one of the 11 rows above.
