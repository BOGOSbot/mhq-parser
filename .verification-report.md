# Points verification — BOGOS Team : 6 dés sous terre (2026-10-10)

Reference: https://mfm.warhammer-community.com/en, cross-checked against the French pages (fr/…) — Munitorum Field Manual v1.5, scraped per faction (20 en + 6 fr pages covering every datasheet in the lists, and the ally pages actually used).

## Method

- 484 unit rows across 35 lists. Each row: (1) datasheet matched to the English MFM page (French roster names translated); (2) printed figure checked for membership in the MFM tier/bracket tables — first/second vs 3rd+ copy pricing, recent ▲/▼ update markers included; (3) on a miss, the delta was checked against the unit's own enhancement (app and newrecruit both fold it into the figure, priced from the MFM detachment lists), then against multiples of the row's MFM wargear-option prices; (4) datasheets with no priced row on /en fall back to the /fr page of the same faction (不正当 the /fr render shows several rows the /en one does not: Doyen en Armure Terminator 75 (▲ +10), Les Lances Jumelles — 2 figurines — 240, Équipe de Cibleurs — 10 figurines — 85, Preneurs de Têtes Gardes Loups = Wolf Guard Headtakers — 3:85 / 6:170).
